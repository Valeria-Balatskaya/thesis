Scan a suspect image

Upload any image (e.g. a screenshot from another site). We check it against every product across every registered seller.
Note: native OS screenshots (Windows Snipping Tool, macOS Cmd+Shift+4) often defeat watermarks due to DPI scaling. Upload the raw file if available.

Product monitoring

Select one of your registered products, then define the URLs where it's supposed to appear (your own store, your Etsy listing, etc). Any match found at an unlisted URL will be flagged as unauthorized — likely stolen.

Authorized locations

Run monitoring scan

Simulate a reverse-image-search crawler having fetched images from various URLs on the web. Upload the candidate images and provide the URLs they were found at (comma-separated, in order).
